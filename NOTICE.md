# Authorship and provenance

Prepared for the academic code portfolio of **Nathaly Henao Lugo**.

The underlying master's research, *Optimización de microdatos de exportaciones con un sistema automatizado y un chatbot de consulta multi agente basado en LLM*, was authored jointly by **Nathaly Henao Lugo, Alejandro Perez Lugo and Mauricio Guzmán Arango**. Nathaly participated across the data-engineering and chatbot phases. This sample preserves the joint authorship.

The principal code excerpts are eight selected methods from the original `AgentCore` and `ConversationSystem` source files. The three agent configurations contain selected original instructions translated to English. Original relative paths, hashes and method ranges are recorded in `docs/SOURCE_MAP.json`.

The selected methods retain the original executable control flow. Formatting, docstrings and specified presentation strings were edited for English review, while compatibility identifiers and lexical patterns were retained. The local demonstration, logging adapter, tests and source map are later review scaffolding; English documentation and packaging were prepared with AI assistance. Those additions do not replace the authorship of the original research or claim that newly added review tools were part of the thesis.

The full thesis, complete project source, institutional records, credentials, original history and deployment configuration are excluded. Illustrative questions are synthetic and are not captured research results. No new NLP performance claims are made.
