# Authorship and provenance

Prepared for the academic code portfolio of **Nathaly Henao Lugo**.

The underlying master's research, *Optimización de microdatos de exportaciones con un sistema automatizado y un chatbot de consulta multi agente basado en LLM*, was authored jointly by **Nathaly Henao Lugo, Alejandro Perez Lugo and Mauricio Guzmán Arango**. The original source provenance includes the [TDG project](https://github.com/AlejoPerez98/TDG). This sample does not change or replace that authorship.

Nathaly contributed across the data-engineering and chatbot components. This separate repository presents selected concepts and a runnable adaptation for academic review, prepared in November 2026 with AI-assisted development and documentation. The reference rules, synthetic records, diagnostic corpus and local execution backend were created for this sample. They should not be represented as the unchanged original thesis code or as independently validated research findings.

The full thesis, production source, deployment settings, original Git history and real institutional records are excluded. The original project remains separate. The demonstration contains no personal export identifiers or live service configuration.

No open-source license has been selected. Please preserve attribution and contact the authors before redistribution or reuse. This notice does not grant rights to institutional data or third-party materials.
